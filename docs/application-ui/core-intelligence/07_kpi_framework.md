# 07. Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Framework
## AI Brand Intelligence & Visibility Platform

This KPI framework defines every performance indicator used across the platform, specifying formulas, visual styles, refresh frequencies, and business meanings to ensure clear and actionable data reporting.

---

## 7.1 Key Performance Indicators

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    PLATFORM KPIs                                        |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                         |
|   1. Share of Model Voice (SoMV) ===> Brand recommendation rate across target LLMs.      |
|   2. Brand Sentiment Index (BSI) ===> Normalized conversational sentiment tone.         |
|   3. Citation Frequency Index    ===> Percentage of direct hyperlink citation references.|
|   4. Entity-Relation Density     ===> Strength of knowledge graph mappings & schemas.   |
|   5. Vector Cosine Similarity    ===> Semantic similarity of content to model embeddings. |
|                                                                                         |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 7.2 Detailed KPI Specifications

### 7.2.1 Share of Model Voice (SoMV)
*   **Purpose**: Measure the brand's overall recommendation share and market presence across target conversational models.
*   **Calculation**:
    $$\text{SoMV} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Recommendations}}{\text{Total Prompts Executed}} \right) \times 100$$
*   **Visualization**: Circular progress ring display showing overall percentage, paired with a multi-line trend chart displaying performance over time.
*   **Refresh Frequency**: Daily (Starter/Growth) or Hourly (Enterprise).
*   **Business Meaning**: Reflects the brand's share of voice and visibility inside conversational search engines.

### 7.2.2 Brand Sentiment Index (BSI)
*   **Purpose**: Evaluate the overall sentiment tone of brand mentions inside generated responses.
*   **Calculation**: A normalized score from -100 (Extremely Negative) to +100 (Extremely Positive) based on parsed brand mention contexts.
*   **Visualization**: Color-coded status badge and horizontal bar chart comparing brand sentiment across platforms.
*   **Refresh Frequency**: Daily.
*   **Business Meaning**: Helps teams identify and resolve brand hallucinations or negative summaries generated by AI platforms.

### 7.2.3 Citation Frequency Index (CFI)
*   **Purpose**: Measure the percentage of generated AI answers containing a direct hyperlink citation back to the brand’s domain.
*   **Calculation**:
    $$\text{CFI} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Direct Citation Links}}{\text{Total Responses with Mentions}} \right) \times 100$$
*   **Visualization**: High-contrast metrics card displaying overall citation percentage and referring domain lists.
*   **Refresh Frequency**: Daily.
*   **Business Meaning**: Measures the performance of citation strategies, helping drive high-intent clicks back to the core site.

### 7.2.4 Entity-Relation Density (ERD)
*   **Purpose**: Evaluate the strength and completeness of the brand's entity mappings across public and regional knowledge bases.
*   **Calculation**: Score from 0 to 100 based on the total number of verified, interconnected semantic attributes mapped to the brand entity (such as company name, parent organization, founders, product lines, and key executives).
*   **Visualization**: Interactive relational trees and node maps displaying entity-relation strengths.
*   **Refresh Frequency**: Weekly.
*   **Business Meaning**: Verifies that AI engines understand the brand's identity and its relation to key industry terms.

### 7.2.5 Vector Cosine Similarity Score
*   **Purpose**: Evaluate how closely our website content matches the semantic retrieval embeddings of target LLMs.
*   **Calculation**: A decimal score from -1.0 to 1.0 representing semantic relevance:
    $$\text{Cosine Similarity} = \frac{\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B}}{\|\mathbf{A}\| \|\mathbf{B}\|}$$
*   **Visualization**: Gauge charts and vector distance maps displaying content similarity.
*   **Refresh Frequency**: On-demand (triggered after content updates) or Monthly.
*   **Business Meaning**: Identifies gaps in content optimization, helping teams write highly optimized articles ready for RAG and AI indexing before publication.
