# 10. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy
## AI Brand Intelligence & Visibility Platform

This Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy outlines our plan to acquire, activate, and grow customers. It details our distribution channels, content programs, educational initiatives, and sales processes, focusing first on our Phase 1 Iran launch before scaling regionally and globally.

## 10.2 Primary Customer Acquisition Channels

### 10.2.1 Educational Inbound Engine (AEO / GEO Focus)
Since we are building a new product category, our marketing must focus heavily on education.
*   **The Blueprint**: Build the most comprehensive educational library for AI Visibility, Generative Engine Optimization, and Answer Engine Optimization.
*   **Key Asset (The Glossary)**: Create a highly detailed, bilingual (Persian & English) dictionary defining key terms like *Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Vector Embeddings, LLM Benchmarking, and Entity Resolution*.
*   **Tactical Execution**: Ensure every blog post is highly optimized to rank well on both traditional Google search and generative engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity, proving our optimization capabilities through our own digital success.

### 10.2.2 High-Intent Diagnostic Tools
*   **The Free AI Visibility Scanner**: A simple, powerful web tool where prospects enter their brand and key products. The scanner simulates a direct query across ChatGPT and Perplexity, providing an instant report on their brand's visibility and sentiment.
*   **The Conversion Strategy**: To see the full report, analyze competitors, and receive actionable optimization steps, users must register with a valid business email, seamlessly moving high-intent leads directly into our sales funnel.

### 10.2.3 Agency Partnership Program
SEO and marketing agencies are natural partners who can scale our reach quickly.
*   **Strategic Enablement**: Launch the **Certified AI Visibility Specialist (CAVS)** program, training agency teams on advanced GEO and AEO methodologies.
*   **Marketing Collateral**: Provide certified agencies with white-labeled audit templates, presentation slides, and pitch decks to help them sell AI visibility audits to their clients.
*   **Shared Growth**: Offer agencies attractive commission structures and multi-workspace management portals, turning agencies into outsourced sales engines for our SaaS platform.

## 10.3 Enterprise Sales Playbook

For high-value enterprises (such as banks, telecom groups, and major retail platforms), we use a high-touch, consultative sales approach.

```
       +-------------------------------------------------------------+
       |                  ENTERPRISE SALES PLAYBOOK                  |
       +-------------------------------------------------------------+
                                      |
                 +--------------------+--------------------+
                 |                                         |
                 v                                         v
+----------------------------------+      +----------------------------------+
|   Step 1: Diagnostic Outreach    |      |    Step 2: Executive Briefing    |
|   - Custom AI Brand Health Audit |      |   - Share findings with C-suite  |
|   - Focus on competitive gaps    |      |   - Present concrete ROI metrics |
+----------------+-----------------+      +----------------+-----------------+
                 |                                         |
                 +--------------------+--------------------+
                                      |
                                      v
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        Step 3: Secure Proof of Concept                     |
|  - 30-day isolated sandbox environment to test, track, and verify results  |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1.  **Diagnostic Outreach**: Our sales team contacts target enterprise CMOs, providing a custom, pre-built **AI Brand Health Audit** that highlights where their brand is currently misrepresented or outpaced by competitors in AI search.
2.  **Executive Briefings**: Arrange a briefing with the client's executive team to present our findings, explain how changing AI search trends affect their market share, and demonstrate the ROI of proactive brand optimization.
3.  **The Proof of Concept (PoC)**: Offer a structured, 30-day proof-of-concept workspace where the enterprise can track their brand, test optimization changes, and verify early success metrics.

## 10.4 Community & Brand Strategy
*   **Specialized Community**: Launch a private, invite-only community for SEO directors, enterprise marketing leaders, and NLP engineers.
*   **Monthly Meetups**: Host regular meetups in major cities (starting with Tehran in Phase 1) to discuss advancements in search, retrieval systems, and generative technology, building deep relationships with key decision-makers.
*   **Open-Source Research**: Publish regular open-source datasets, language model evaluation tools, and benchmark studies, positioning our platform as the technical leader in regional NLP and generative search.
