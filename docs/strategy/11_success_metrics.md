# 11. Product Success Metrics
## AI Brand Intelligence & Visibility Platform

This document defines our product success metrics, establishing a clear framework to measure business health, platform adoption, and user value. By tracking a clear North Star Metric alongside key SaaS performance indicators (KPIs), we align our product development with customer value.

---

## 11.1 The North Star Metric

> ### **Weekly Active Optimized Queries (WAOQ)**
> *The total number of brand tracking and prompt optimization queries executed across all active user workspaces where the user has successfully implemented at least one recommended technical optimization (e.g., entity schema injection or content update).*

### 11.1.1 Why this is our North Star Metric
*   **Reflects Real Value**: Measuring simple page views or dashboard logins does not prove that users are getting value. WAOQ only grows when users are actively tracking prompts *and* acting on the platform's optimization recommendations.
*   **Drives Customer Retention**: Users who implement our technical recommendations see measurable improvements in their AI visibility, directly driving long-term retention and subscription renewals.
*   **Scales with Account Value**: Larger enterprise customers track thousands of queries across multiple brands, making this metric an accurate predictor of revenue expansion and platform adoption.

## 11.3 Detailed KPI Definitions & Target Goals

### 11.3.1 Acquisition Metrics
*   **Organic Search & AI Impressions**: The total number of organic impressions our educational guides and glossary pages receive across Google and generative search engines.
*   **Free Scanner Conversion Rate**: The percentage of website visitors who use our Free AI Visibility Scanner and complete a registration to unlock their full audit report.
    *   *Target Goal*: **> 12%** conversion rate from scanner usage to trial registration.

### 11.3.2 Activation Metrics
*   **Successful Onboarding Rate**: The percentage of newly registered users who complete the three-step onboarding sequence within their first 48 hours:
    1. Set up their core Brand Entity profile.
    2. Add at least 1 competitor brand.
    3. Run their first multi-model tracking campaign.
    *   *Target Goal*: **> 65%** onboarding completion rate.

### 11.3.3 Retention Metrics
*   **Monthly Active Workspace Retention**: The percentage of user workspaces that log in and execute a tracking query at least once a month.
*   **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: The percentage of recurring revenue retained from existing customers over a given period, including expansions and upgrades, minus downgrades and churn.
    *   *Target Goal*: **> 115%** annual NRR for mid-market and enterprise cohorts.
*   **Churn Rate**: The percentage of monthly subscribers who cancel their plans.
    *   *Target Goal*: **< 2.5%** monthly churn on Starter/Growth plans; **< 0.5%** annual churn on Enterprise contracts.

### 11.3.4 Revenue Metrics
*   **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: The total predictable recurring revenue generated by our active subscription plans.
*   **Customer Lifetime Value to Customer Acquisition Cost Ratio (LTV:CAC)**: The financial efficiency of our customer acquisition model.
    *   *Target Goal*: **> 4:1** LTV:CAC ratio.

### 11.3.5 Product Usage & Engagement Metrics
*   **Recommendations Implementation Rate**: The percentage of recommendations generated by our AI agent that are marked as resolved by our users.
    *   *Target Goal*: **> 40%** implementation rate within 30 days of recommendation generation.
*   **Report Export Frequency**: The average number of automated PDF visibility reports exported or scheduled per workspace each month.
    *   *Target Goal*: **> 2.5** reports exported per active workspace monthly.
