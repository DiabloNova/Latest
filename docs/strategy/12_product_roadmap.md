## 12.2 Detailed Development Milestones

### 12.2.1 Phase 1A: Iran Core Release (Months 1–6)
*   **Primary Focus**: Launch our specialized Persian-language AI visibility tracking system and build initial market authority in Iran.
*   **Key Deliverables**:
    *   **Core Platform Scaffolding**: Build our high-performance Next.js application framework with native, bilingual RTL/LTR layout toggles.
    *   **Persian NLP Tokenizer**: Develop and deploy our specialized tokenizers and sentiment-analysis engines optimized for Persian character sets.
    *   **The Free AI Visibility Scanner**: Launch our web-based diagnostic tool to drive low-friction lead generation and user acquisition.
    *   **Basic AI Tracking Engine**: Build tracking pipelines for top conversational models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) supporting manual, multi-prompt test campaigns.
    *   **Unified Brand Workspace**: Design and build the primary user dashboard, allowing customers to easily track brand mentions, view basic Share of Voice charts, and export simple PDF reports.

---

### 12.2.2 Phase 1B: Enterprise Scale & Automation (Months 7–12)
*   **Primary Focus**: Scale the platform's automation capabilities, target large enterprise customers, and build partner channels.
*   **Key Deliverables**:
    *   **Automated Schema Generator**: Build an interactive tool that automatically generates high-density JSON-LD schema files based on a client's brand profile.
    *   **The AI Optimization Agent**: Launch our automated recommendation engine, providing users with explicit, step-by-step content changes to improve their AI visibility.
    *   **White-Label Agency Portals**: Release our specialized agency workspaces, enabling partners to generate custom, white-labeled PDF reports for their clients.
    *   **Custom RAG Simulator**: Build a premium enterprise tool allowing clients to upload internal company documentation and test how private RAG systems respond to query prompts.
    *   **Local Payment Gateway**: Integrate localized payment processors in Iran, enabling seamless enterprise billing in Tomans and automated invoice generation.

---

### 12.2.3 Phase 2: Regional GCC Expansion (Months 13–24)
*   **Primary Focus**: Expand our market footprint into GCC countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar) and adapt our platform to regional enterprise needs.
*   **Key Deliverables**:
    *   **Arabic NLP Engine**: Deploy advanced tokenizers and analysis pipelines optimized for Arabic dialects and regional spelling variations.
    *   **Multi-Region Scraper Array**: Distribute our scraper nodes across regional cloud environments to guarantee low-latency simulations from multiple Middle Eastern locations.
    *   **SSO & Corporate Compliance**: Integrate enterprise Single Sign-On (SSO) systems (SAML, Okta, Active Directory) and align with regional data privacy laws.
    *   **Localized Agency Enablement**: Launch custom, regional agency sub-domain portals, allowing regional partners to manage multi-lingual client portfolios.
    *   **Regional Citation Tracking**: Build monitoring tools to track brand citations across regional news outlets and localized search engines.

---

### 12.2.4 Phase 3: Global Scaling (Months 25–36+)
*   **Primary Focus**: Scale into a globally recognized enterprise SaaS platform, competing directly with international brand intelligence tools.
*   **Key Deliverables**:
    *   **Global Prompt Network**: Deploy a globally distributed network of prompt-testing nodes to guarantee precise localized simulations from any region.
    *   **Global Enterprise Integrations**: Launch pre-built integrations with major global systems, including HubSpot, Salesforce, Adobe Experience Manager, and Shopify.
    *   **Advanced Content Optimizer**: Build an in-app content editor that analyzes text in real-time, providing immediate recommendations to maximize semantic density and vector similarity.
    *   **SOC2 & ISO Certification**: Complete comprehensive SOC2 Type II and ISO 27001 audits, validating our platform's data security and operational integrity for global enterprise procurement.
    *   **Venture Expansion Capital**: Leverage our established regional revenue and technical platform success to secure global venture capital funding to accelerate global marketing and team growth.
