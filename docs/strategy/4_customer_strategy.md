# 4. Customer Strategy
## AI Brand Intelligence & Visibility Platform

This customer strategy defines our Ideal Customer Profiles (ICP) across six key segments. By understanding the distinct business characteristics, key pain points, buying motivations, budget structures, and success criteria for each segment, our sales and product teams can deliver targeted, high-impact value.

### 4.1.1 Segment A: Enterprise Companies
*   **Business Characteristics**: Large organizations with multi-million dollar revenues, extensive digital footprints, complex brand portfolios, and structured corporate hierarchies (e.g., Banking, Telecom, Logistics, Petroleum, Retail conglomerates).
*   **Key Problems**:
    *   **Inaccurate AI Narratives**: AI engines presenting outdated, misleading, or incorrect financial and executive data.
    *   **Loss of Market Share**: Customers asking AI models for the "best enterprise services" and being directed to competitors.
    *   **Lack of Control**: Compliance, legal, and public relations departments have no visibility into how the company is described across AI platforms.
*   **Buying Motivation**: Risk mitigation, brand protection, and maintaining market dominance.
*   **Budget Expectations**: Premium enterprise contracts ($5,000 - $25,000+ USD/month equivalent or 250M - 1B+ Tomans/month during Phase 1).
*   **Success Criteria**: Stable real-time sentiment alerts, complete brand control across main AI models, multi-tenant workspace security, and deep API capabilities.

---

### 4.1.2 Segment B: E-commerce Companies
*   **Business Characteristics**: Large online stores and marketplaces containing thousands of product SKUs, relying heavily on organic search and product discovery channels.
*   **Key Problems**:
    *   **Missed Product Recommendations**: Missing out on recommendations when users ask AI tools for specific product comparisons or shopping advice (e.g., "Recommend the best Persian-designed leather bags under 3 million Tomans").
    *   **Lack of Product Citations**: LLMs sourcing product data from low-quality external forums rather than direct store indices.
*   **Buying Motivation**: Direct revenue growth, higher product conversion rates, and securing prominent placement in conversational shopping lists.
*   **Budget Expectations**: High-tier SaaS plans ($1,000 - $3,500 USD/month equivalent or 50M - 180M Tomans/month).
*   **Success Criteria**: High visibility across e-commerce searches on Perplexity and Gemini, and accurate SKU schema optimization metrics.

---

### 4.1.3 Segment C: SaaS Companies
*   **Business Characteristics**: Modern B2B and B2C software providers seeking to scale their product adoption through digital discovery and educational marketing.
*   **Key Problems**:
    *   **Missing from AI Grids**: Not being included when AI systems generate software comparison grids (e.g., "Compare the top CRM platforms in the Middle East").
    *   **Feature Discovery Gaps**: AI models misunderstanding specialized software features and recommending competitor tools instead.
*   **Buying Motivation**: Lower customer acquisition costs (CAC), rapid market positioning, and higher product sign-ups.
*   **Budget Expectations**: Growth to Enterprise tiers ($500 - $2,000 USD/month equivalent or 25M - 100M Tomans/month).
*   **Success Criteria**: Prominent inclusion in AI software comparisons and high CTR from citation links back to core product pages.

---

### 4.1.4 Segment D: Digital Marketing & SEO Agencies
*   **Business Characteristics**: Agencies managing digital strategy, brand visibility, and organic growth for multiple corporate clients.
*   **Key Problems**:
    *   **Service commoditization**: Traditional SEO service values are dropping; agencies need new, high-margin strategic offerings.
    *   **Client Retention**: Clients asking for AEO/GEO reports and threatening to leave for AI-focused agencies if these services are not provided.
*   **Buying Motivation**: Creating new high-margin service streams, protecting client retention rates, and utilizing white-label reporting features.
*   **Budget Expectations**: Professional and Custom agency tiers ($800 - $4,000 USD/month equivalent or 40M - 200M Tomans/month).
*   **Success Criteria**: Professional white-labeled PDF visibility reports, multi-client workspace portals, and API dashboard integration.

---

### 4.1.5 Segment E: Startups
*   **Business Characteristics**: Fast-growing, venture-backed companies that need to build brand footprint and market authority rapidly with limited initial capital.
*   **Key Problems**:
    *   **No Brand Footprint in LLM Indices**: Generative systems have never heard of their brand because they were trained on older datasets.
    *   **Struggling against established brands**: Traditional search engine keywords are dominated by legacy players with huge backlink profiles.
*   **Buying Motivation**: Accelerating market entry, building brand awareness, and bypassing traditional search barriers through GEO tactics.
*   **Budget Expectations**: Starter to Growth tiers ($150 - $450 USD/month equivalent or 7M - 22M Tomans/month).
*   **Success Criteria**: Fast indexing in model datasets, entity creation in local databases, and measurable growth in Share of Model Voice.

---

### 4.1.6 Segment F: Local Businesses
*   **Business Characteristics**: Local physical service providers, clinics, restaurants, and retail spaces relying on proximity and local search queries.
*   **Key Problems**:
    *   **Excluded from Local AI Recommendations**: Missing out on recommendations when users search for local services (e.g., "Recommend a trusted dental clinic near Jordan street, Tehran").
    *   **Outdated Business Details**: AI platforms sharing incorrect operating hours, physical locations, or contact numbers.
*   **Buying Motivation**: Securing consistent local foot traffic and customer phone inquiries.
*   **Budget Expectations**: Entry-level tiers ($49 - $149 USD/month equivalent or 2.5M - 7.5M Tomans/month).
*   **Success Criteria**: Consistent placement in localized AI searches, and error-free contact information across conversational answers.
