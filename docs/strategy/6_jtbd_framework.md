# 6. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) Framework
## AI Brand Intelligence & Visibility Platform

The Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) framework helps us understand the fundamental functional, emotional, and social progress our buyers are trying to achieve. Instead of focusing on product features, this framework looks at the core "jobs" our customers are hiring our platform to perform.
