# 9. Pricing Strategy

This pricing strategy defines our monetization structure across all three phases of growth. By combining low-friction, self-service entry points with premium enterprise tiers and localized regional billing options, we maximize early adoption while building a highly profitable, recurring software business.

## 9.2 Phase 1: Localized Iran Pricing Plans (Monthly)

To capture the local market quickly, our Phase 1 plans are priced and billed in Tomans, aligning with regional enterprise procurement standards.

### 9.2.1 Starter Plan
*   **Target Audience**: Startups and growing local businesses.
*   **Price**: **4,900,000 Tomans / Month** (Approx. $100 USD equivalent).
*   **Core Inclusions**:
    *   1 Active Workspace.
    *   Track up to 2 Brands / Competitors.
    *   250 Tracked Queries / Prompts per month.
    *   Daily updates on 2 major AI systems (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini).
    *   Standard email support.

### 9.2.2 Growth Plan
*   **Target Audience**: Mid-sized e-commerce stores and corporate marketing teams.
*   **Price**: **19,500,000 Tomans / Month** (Approx. $400 USD equivalent).
*   **Core Inclusions**:
    *   3 Active Workspaces.
    *   Track up to 5 Brands / Competitors.
    *   1,500 Tracked Queries / Prompts per month.
    *   Updates across all major AI systems (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity).
    *   Automated Weekly PDF Reports.
    *   Priority email and chat support (Response under 4 hours).

### 9.2.3 Agency Plan
*   **Target Audience**: Digital marketing and specialized SEO agencies.
*   **Price**: **49,000,000 Tomans / Month** (Approx. $1,000 USD equivalent).
*   **Core Inclusions**:
    *   15 Active Workspaces (Dedicated client portfolios).
    *   Track up to 25 Brands / Competitors.
    *   5,000 Tracked Queries / Prompts per month.
    *   White-labeled reporting (Add agency logos and custom color schemes).
    *   API Access (Read-only data endpoints).
    *   Dedicated Account Manager.

### 9.2.4 Enterprise Plan (Bespoke Contracts)
*   **Target Audience**: Large enterprises, banks, telecom groups, and conglomerates.
*   **Price**: **Custom Pricing (Starting from 150,000,000 Tomans / Month)**.
*   **Core Inclusions**:
    *   Unlimited Workspaces and Brands.
    *   Custom Tracked Query volumes (Over 10,000+ prompts/month).
    *   Custom, real-time alerting pipelines.
    *   Bespoke RAG simulation environments (Private document testing).
    *   Dedicated Single Sign-On (SSO/SAML) setups.
    *   24/7 SLA Support with dedicated technical account manager.

## 9.4 Trial & Free Acquisition Strategies

### 9.4.1 Free Tier (Value-First Lead Generation)
We do not offer a fully free ongoing dashboard plan. Instead, we provide a **Free AI Visibility Scanner**.
*   **The Hook**: Users enter their company name and a primary product query.
*   **The Value**: The tool runs a live, single-prompt simulation across ChatGPT and Perplexity, displaying a basic brand mention rating.
*   **The Conversion**: To see the full sentiment score, competitor comparison, and actionable optimization steps, the user must sign up for a free trial using a business email.

### 9.4.2 Free Trial Strategy
*   **Duration**: 14-day free trial available on the Starter and Growth tiers.
*   **Requirements**: Business email registration required. Credit card/payment details are optional during Phase 1 (to maximize local signup velocity), but required for global tiers.
*   **Onboarding**: During the trial, automated emails and in-app tooltips guide the user to perform three high-value actions:
    1. Set up their core Brand Entity profile.
    2. Add their top 3 competitors.
    3. Generate their first automated AI Visibility PDF Report.

## 9.5 Enterprise Contract Architecture
Enterprise contracts are built on a multi-year subscription model (typically 12 to 24 months) with structured payment terms.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                               ENTERPRISE CONTRACT COMPONENTS                            |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                                         |
|   [Core Subscription Fee]  ===>  [Telemetry Scaling Fee]  ===>  [Strategic Consulting]  |
|   - Base platform access         - Volumetric prompt fees       - Team training workshops|
|   - SLA, SSO, Account Manager    - Custom scrapers / pipelines  - Bespoke entity audits  |
|                                                                                         |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

1.  **Core Platform Fee**: Annual base subscription covering security compliance, SSO integration, platform SLA, and dedicated Account Management.
2.  **Telemetry Scaling Fees**: Volumetric pricing based on the total number of tracked queries, custom API integrations, and the frequency of scraper runs.
3.  **Bespoke Services Add-on**: Optional enterprise consulting services, including custom knowledge graph alignment, regional public relations monitoring, and team training workshops.
