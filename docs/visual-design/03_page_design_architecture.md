# 03. Page Design Architecture
## AI Brand Intelligence & Visibility Platform

This document defines the page design templates for 12 key marketing pages. Each template specifies the hero strategy, content structure, CTA placement, and trust element rules.

---

## 3.1 Marketing Page Visual Blueprints

### 3.1.1 Homepage
*   **Hero Strategy**: Center-aligned, minimalist vertical stack. Large headings (bold, 48px), secondary supporting descriptions, and split primary/secondary action buttons.
*   **Content Structure**: Funnel structure (Hero -> Trust Logos -> Problem -> Platform -> Bento Grid -> Testimonials -> CTA Form).
*   **CTA Placement**: High-contrast split actions in the hero; interactive scanner form in the final conversion section.
*   **Trust Elements**: Client logo cloud (slate-gray) and detailed customer success card grids.

### 3.1.2 Platform
*   **Hero Strategy**: Left-aligned split columns layout. Left column displays the value proposition; right column displays our **AI Processing Pipeline** visualizer.
*   **Content Structure**: Architectural overview focusing on multi-model scraping, normalized data stores, and deep telemetry layers.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Explore Features" button in the hero; "Contact Sales" banner at page bottom.
*   **Trust Elements**: Complete system status indicators and security compliance badges (SOC2, GDPR).

### 3.1.3 Features
*   **Hero Strategy**: Large, centered heading with an interactive, scroll-triggered bento showcase of key system features.
*   **Content Structure**: Individual sections detailing features (Visibility Monitoring, Competitor Tracking, Reporting).
*   **CTA Placement**: Inline CTAs for specific features (e.g., "Schedule PDF Demo" next to the reporting showcase).
*   **Trust Elements**: Inline product screenshots demonstrating real-time dashboard data.

### 3.1.4 Solutions
*   **Hero Strategy**: Centered title, supported by segment selectors (E-commerce, SaaS, Enterprise, Agencies).
*   **Content Structure**: Customer-centric sections detailing specific problems, buying motivations, and success criteria for each segment.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Book a Personalized Demo" forms tailored to each target segment.
*   **Trust Elements**: Segment-specific case studies (e.g., An e-commerce success story on the E-commerce solutions page).

### 3.1.5 Services
*   **Hero Strategy**: Clean heading, supported by horizontal cards detailing our service pillars (AEO, GEO, AI SEO, and Entity Optimization).
*   **Content Structure**: Highly technical sections detailing on-page audits, schema creations, and Wikidata integrations.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Get a Free Service Audit" dynamic forms.
*   **Trust Elements**: Case studies demonstrating concrete brand visibility and citation increases.

### 3.1.6 Pricing
*   **Hero Strategy**: Centered, bold title, supported by an interactive monthly/annual billing cycle toggle.
*   **Content Structure**: Multi-currency pricing grids, comprehensive plan feature matrices, and FAQ blocks.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Start Free Trial" buttons inside the Starter, Growth, and Agency pricing cards; "Contact Sales" button inside the Enterprise card.
*   **Trust Elements**: Lock icons displaying secure payment gateways, and local currency indicators (Tomans in Phase 1).

### 3.1.7 Case Studies
*   **Hero Strategy**: Left-aligned headline, showcasing a featured case study with high-contrast analytics metrics.
*   **Content Structure**: Grids of case study cards filterable by industry and target platform.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Read Full Study" buttons inside each card; "Get Similar Success" banner at page bottom.
*   **Trust Elements**: Verified customer quotes and verified performance data.

### 3.1.8 Customers
*   **Hero Strategy**: Centered title, supported by a rich grid of customer testimonial cards and review badges.
*   **Content Structure**: Detailed customer success profiles, filterable by business vertical.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Join Our Satisfied Clients" CTAs at page bottom.
*   **Trust Elements**: Client corporate logos and direct links to G2 or Capterra rating portals.

### 3.1.9 Resources
*   **Hero Strategy**: Large centered search input, allowing users to search our guides, templates, and bilingual glossary.
*   **Content Structure**: Clean categories grid (Guides, Checklists, Templates, Videos).
*   **CTA Placement**: "Download PDF Checklist" or "Use Free Tool" CTAs inside resource cards.
*   **Trust Elements**: Verified research references and expert contributor bios.

### 3.1.10 Blog
*   **Hero Strategy**: Left-aligned split column. Left side displays the featured article; right side displays a newsletter subscription box.
*   **Content Structure**: Grid of recent articles, categorized by semantic topic clusters.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Subscribe to Newsletter" and "Read Article" action triggers.
*   **Trust Elements**: Read-time indicators and expert author bios.

### 3.1.11 About
*   **Hero Strategy**: Centered title, supported by a chronological storyline mapping our platform's expansion.
*   **Content Structure**: Company history, executive leadership profiles, and open career listings.
*   **CTA Placement**: "View Open Roles" or "Contact Us" buttons.
*   **Trust Elements**: Executive team headshots and links to our research whitepapers.

### 3.1.12 Contact
*   **Hero Strategy**: Centered title, supported by a 2-column layout. Left side displays direct contact info and addresses; right side displays our Sales Inquiry Form.
*   **Content Structure**: Clean map displays, local operating hours, and sales telephone lines.
*   **CTA Placement**: "Submit Form" button.
*   **Trust Elements**: Privacy declarations and secure data processing icons.
