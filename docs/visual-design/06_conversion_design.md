# 06. Conversion Design
## AI Brand Intelligence & Visibility Platform

This conversion design strategy defines our lead-generation funnels, call-to-action (CTA) placements, and targeted registration flows across our core customer segments.

---

## 6.2 Targeted CTA Strategies

To maximize conversion rates, we customize calls-to-action for each primary customer segment:

### 6.2.1 Segment A: Enterprise Visitors (CMOs / VP of Marketing)
*   **Buying Motivation**: Brand protection, risk mitigation, and Board-level reporting.
*   **Primary CTA**: "Book a Personalized Enterprise Demo."
*   **Landing Page Element**: A direct, high-touch calendar booking form (HubSpot/Calendly integration) connected to our sales pipeline.
*   **Value Hook**: Offering a "Custom AI Brand Reputation Audit" report.

### 6.2.2 Segment B: Tech Startups & Software Providers (CEOs)
*   **Buying Motivation**: Lower customer acquisition costs (CAC) and rapid market indexing.
*   **Primary CTA**: "Start 14-Day Free Trial."
*   **Landing Page Element**: Low-friction signup panel requiring only a business email and workspace name.
*   **Value Hook**: Free, self-service sandbox access to track their brand instantly.

### 6.2.3 Segment C: Digital Marketing Agencies (Agency Owners)
*   **Buying Motivation**: Retaining clients, proving campaign value, and high-margin upsells.
*   **Primary CTA**: "Get Agency Partner Kit."
*   **Landing Page Element**: Downloads containing white-labeled reporting templates and pitch deck outlines.
*   **Value Hook**: Access to multi-client workspace portals and custom branding tools.

### 6.2.4 Segment D: Technical Specialists (SEO Managers / Developers)
*   **Buying Motivation**: Accurate technical data, API integrations, and custom schema configurations.
*   **Primary CTA**: "Access Developer Sandbox."
*   **Landing Page Element**: A free-to-use schema.org builder tool.
*   **Value Hook**: Free test API keys and open-source validation models.

## 6.3 Low-Friction Onboarding Forms
*   **Progressive Form Fields**: Forms use progressive disclosure rules. The initial scanner form requires only a website URL and primary search query. Once submitted, the system displays an animated loading bar and asks for a business email to unlock the final report, minimizing drop-offs.
*   **Social Sign-In CTAs**: Offer secure single-tap OAuth options (Google, GitHub, and local enterprise systems) to streamline workspace creations.
*   **Contextual Assistance**: Form fields include inline helper text and hover tooltips explaining *why* specific information is required, reassuring users on data privacy.
